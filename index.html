Texas Scramble

Enter team handicaps and gross scores to calculate net scores and rankings.

1. Choose team format

4-player team handicap = 25% of lowest + 20% of second-lowest + 15% of third-lowest + 10% of highest course handicap.

In 4-player mode, enter handicaps for either 3 or 4 players. If only 3 handicap fields are filled, the team handicap is calculated using 25% / 20% / 15%. If all 4 are filled, it uses 25% / 20% / 15% / 10%, from lowest to highest. Two-player teams use 35% of the lower + 15% of the higher handicap. Handicaps show 2 decimals. Net score = gross team score − team handicap.

2. Enter teams

No teams yet. Choose a format and press “Add Team.”

Enter each player's course handicap for the tees being played. Enter the team's total gross score after the round. Blank scores are not included in the leaderboard.
